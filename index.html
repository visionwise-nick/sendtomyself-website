Your Information, Always With You

This is not just a simple "file transfer tool", but a personal information hub. It solves the core problems of "information fragmentation" and "device isolation" in modern digital life. Cloud sync, no login required, multi-device support, permanent memory—no matter which device you have in hand, your information is always with you.

Core Value Proposition

Send to Myself stores everything in the cloud, making it easy to sync historical memories and messages when you switch phones or devices. Record and sync to all your devices anytime, anywhere (no matter which device you have), with simple usage that requires no account maintenance (no login required, device-based identification). Multiple devices can form multiple groups for different purposes (all your personal devices, all family member devices, all work partner devices, etc.). As long as you use Send to Myself, your information is always with you and ready to use.

Three Core Scenarios

Make information truly your second brain

1

The First App to Install on Your New Device

Switching phones/computers is no longer "moving", but "coming home"

When users buy a new phone or computer, the biggest headache is how to seamlessly migrate scattered but important information (links, notes, temporary files) from the old device. The old methods are painful: email, data cables, logging into various cloud drives...

📱
Open Send to Myself on the new device, scan the QR code on the old device
⚡
Instantly, all historical messages, files, and "memories" are perfectly displayed on the new device
🔐
No login required, scan and use. Cloud sync, permanent storage, never lose
2

One Space, All Roles

Create dedicated information channels for your different life circles

Our digital lives are multifaceted, but tools often mix everything together. Work files, family photos, personal inspiration... all crammed into one place.

👤
Group: "My Devices" - Personal productivity space, code snippets, to-dos, important links
👨‍👩‍👧
Group: "Family Album" - Family emotional space, children's photos, family dinner videos
💼
Group: "Side Project Team" - Team collaboration space, design drafts, documents, discussions

Information is organized, no more chaos. Supports all devices: iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux.

3

Information Follows You, Not Trapped by Devices

No matter which device you have, your "second brain" is always ready

You see important information on your phone while on the road, want to save it to process on your home computer, but forget when you get home. Or, critical files are stored on your desktop, but you can't access them when you're out and need them urgently.

📸
Capture - Take a photo of a passage from a book in a café with your phone, send it to Send to Myself, and tap "★ Add to Memory"
🔄
Sync - Almost simultaneously, the image and text appear in the Send to Myself app on your laptop screen next to you
🔍
Access - On another computer in the office, use the "Memory" filter function, enter keywords, and instantly find the image saved months ago

Record on the go, all devices sync instantly. "Add to Memory" permanently saves important information. Supports all file types: text, links, images, videos, documents...
